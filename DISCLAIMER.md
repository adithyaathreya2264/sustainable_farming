# Disclaimer

**Chiguru Nele is an academic project. Its advice is not verified by agricultural experts and is not a substitute for them.**

## What this means

- **Advice is unverified.** The alerts and recommendations come from draft agronomic rules that no agronomist, Krishi Vigyan Kendra (KVK) or ICAR scientist has signed off. Rule-based advice is marked **"[Unverified guidance]"** in the app.
- **Model results are computer estimates.** Every machine-learning result (crop suggestions, nutrient targets, soil fertility, yield outlook, photo checks, price outlook) is provisional. Several models were trained on lab or curated photos or on synthetic or unverified data, and may be wrong on real fields. The app does not show probabilities, and a result is never "verified".
- **Photo checks can be wrong.** A result means "this looks most like...", not a diagnosis. Some checks cannot recognise a healthy plant, and none can recognise a problem outside the classes it was trained on. Always compare with the plant in front of you.
- **Government scheme matches are provisional.** A match does not mean you are eligible. Check the official scheme page and your local agriculture office before applying.
- **Prices and yield are indicative.** The price outlook covers Karnataka only and has not been tested for every commodity. Yield is shown only as a comparison with past records, never as a quantity. Neither is a guarantee or a reason to sell, hold or plant.
- **Land-unit conversions** other than standard units and the Karnataka Guntha are unverified.
- **Weather data** comes from a third-party service and may be inaccurate or unavailable.

## Your responsibility

- Consult your local agriculture officer, KVK, or a qualified agronomist before acting on any advice, especially before buying or applying fertilizer, pesticide or other chemicals.
- Follow the product label and local regulations for any chemical.
